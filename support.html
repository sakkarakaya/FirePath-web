Support and Contact

Help with the FirePath mobile app and website.

Contact Support

Report a problem, ask a question or suggest an improvement through the FirePath support tracker. Please include the app version, device model and steps that reproduce the issue.

Open a support request

Support requests on GitHub are public. Never include income, account details, portfolio values, exported files or other personal or sensitive information.

Before You Contact Support

  • Install the latest available version of FirePath.
  • Close and reopen the app if a screen stops responding.
  • Check that the values and currency in Settings match the plan you intended to enter.
  • Do not delete or reset data before exporting anything you want to keep.

Data and Privacy

FirePath stores app data locally on your device. To export or erase it, open FirePath and go to Settings → Export and reset. On the website, use the same section or clear this site's stored data in your browser. FirePath has no server-side account to recover after local data is erased.

Financial Questions

Support can explain how a FirePath feature or calculation works, but it cannot provide personalized investment, tax, legal or financial advice. Consult a qualified professional for advice about your circumstances.
