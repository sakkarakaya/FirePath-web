Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Overview

FirePath is a local-first educational FIRE planning tool. The mobile app and website provide financial calculations, manual inputs and learning content. FirePath does not provide investment, tax, legal or financial advice.

Information You Enter

You may enter financial planning information such as income, expenses, assets, debts, portfolio holdings and FIRE assumptions. In the FirePath mobile app, this information is stored locally on your device. On this website, it is stored in your browser's local storage. FirePath does not transmit quantities, purchase prices, portfolio totals or profile inputs to its optional market data service.

Information FirePath Collects

FirePath does not require an account and does not collect personal information or financial inputs from the app. It does not connect to banks, brokerages, advertising networks or analytics services. If you enable optional market data on the website, instrument search text, ticker and exchange identifiers, and requested currency pairs are sent to the configured FirePath proxy and its market data provider to return search results and prices. FirePath does not use this information for tracking or targeted advertising.

Service Providers and Technical Data

FirePath does not sell or share your personal information. Apple may process information when you download or update the mobile app under Apple's own privacy terms. This website is hosted through GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs to provide and secure the hosting service. FirePath does not receive your financial inputs through either provider.

Optional website market data is supplied by Twelve Data through a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare and Twelve Data may process technical request information such as IP addresses, request logs and the instrument identifiers you request under their respective privacy terms. The market data feature is not used by the mobile app and can be left disconnected on the website.

Support Requests

If you choose to open a support request through GitHub, GitHub processes your account information and the content you submit under its own privacy terms. Support requests are public and may remain in the repository's issue history. FirePath uses that information only to understand and respond to the request. Do not submit financial or other sensitive information.

Storage, Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device or in your browser until you remove it. In the mobile app, use Settings → Export and reset to erase local data, or delete the app. On the website, use Settings → Export and reset or clear this site's stored data in your browser. Device backups may retain copies according to your operating system or backup provider settings.

Your Privacy Choices

Because FirePath has no account or server-side profile, there is no server-held FirePath account data to access, correct or delete. You can review, change, export and erase the information stored locally by using the app or website controls. If the product's data practices change, this policy and the relevant consent choices will be updated before the new processing begins where required.

Children

FirePath is not intended for children under 16, and FirePath does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when FirePath changes. The latest version should be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the FirePath support page. Do not include financial account details, portfolio values or other sensitive information in a public support request.

FirePath provides educational information and financial calculations only. It does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice. Always do your own research or consult a qualified professional.
