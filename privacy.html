Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

FirePath is designed as a local-first educational FIRE planning tool. The mobile app and this website focus on financial calculations, manual inputs and learning content. FirePath does not provide investment, tax, legal or financial advice.

Information You Enter

You may enter financial planning information such as income, expenses, assets, debts, portfolio holdings and FIRE assumptions. In the FirePath mobile app, this information is stored locally on your device. On this website, calculator and portfolio demo data may be stored in your browser using local storage so your inputs can remain available on the same device and browser.

No Accounts or External Finance APIs

FirePath does not require an account for the local-first MVP. It does not connect to banks, brokerages, market data providers or external financial APIs.

Data Sharing

FirePath does not sell your personal information. FirePath does not knowingly share your financial inputs with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. If you publish or host this website through GitHub Pages, GitHub may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and server request logs as part of hosting the website.

Your Choices

You can clear website data by using your browser settings for this site. You can remove mobile app data by using in-app data controls where available or by deleting the app from your device.

Children

FirePath is not intended for children. It is an educational financial planning tool for users who can evaluate financial assumptions responsibly.

Changes

This policy may be updated when FirePath changes. The latest version should be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact listed on the App Store or Google Play listing for FirePath.

FirePath provides educational information and financial calculations only. It does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice. Always do your own research or consult a qualified professional.
